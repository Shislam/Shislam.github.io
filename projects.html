CANSLIM is an investing methodology developed by William O'Neal that takes a series of fundamentals and technicals off a stock to determine whether or not it's likely to make a large move over the course of several weeks. One of the tests he uses involves counting the number of "distribution days" that a major market index undergoes to determine if the market is in a bear or bull cycle.

This indicator was coded with pinescript on tradingview and is more a work in progress. It hasn't been great at identifying market movements thusfar, but the moving average lines that I added to it have been useful for me. The free version of TradingView only allows for a set number of indicators, but by slapping multiple indicators and lines in one coded indicator, I can get around the limitation.

At present, my main strategy involves selling when stocks cross below the 50 day simple moving average, so this composite indicator has been helpful in that regard.
